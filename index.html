About

Images that say something

Uncle Eruted Photography is built on a simple belief: an image should do more than document a moment. It should carry a feeling, tell a story and give people something worth keeping.

Led by Dr. David Okuneye and based in Bodija, Ibadan since 2021, we photograph portraits, birthdays, convocations, weddings, corporate events and products for clients across Ibadan, Lagos and Abeokuta.

Whether it's one person, a couple, a family or a whole organisation, the goal is the same: considered, authentic, beautiful work, and a shoot you actually enjoy.

FAQ

Good to know

Where is your studio?

Our studio is at 9 Elder Adedokun Close, Ashi-Bodija Road, Bodija, Ibadan, Oyo, near Deeper Life Bible Church, Ashi. We also shoot on location anywhere in Ibadan, and travel to Lagos and Abeokuta.

How do I book?

Send a WhatsApp message to 0807 095 3724 with your date and the type of shoot. A 70% deposit confirms the booking.

How soon do I get my photos?

Portrait sessions are delivered in about 3 days. Events and weddings take about 2 weeks.

I'm not photogenic. Will my photos still look good?

Almost everyone says this. We direct every pose, angle and expression, so you only have to show up and enjoy it.
